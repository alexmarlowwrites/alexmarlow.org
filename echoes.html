Paper & Echo

The Echoes

A place for the words that have nowhere else to go.

Leave it here.

The Echoes is a collection of anonymous thoughts, stories, confessions, memories, and truths that deserve somewhere to exist.

No names. No profiles. No need to explain who you are. Just the words you need to get out.

You can leave things like...

  • A thought you've never said out loud.
  • A memory you still carry.
  • A confession you've never made.
  • A letter you will never send.
  • Something you're still trying to understand.
  • A story that deserves to exist somewhere.
  • Something you simply need to get off your chest.

Why anonymous?

Because names can make us careful.

Sometimes we change the way we tell the truth when we know someone is going to know it belongs to us.

Anonymity gives us room to be honest.

The Echoes is not about knowing who said it. It is about knowing that someone said it.
