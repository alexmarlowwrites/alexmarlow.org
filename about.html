The Writer

About

Alex Marlow is for the things we are afraid to say.

The things we keep to ourselves because they feel too vulnerable, too complicated, or too difficult to explain. The things we don't realize need to be said until years later, when we finally have the words for them.

Alex writes about memory, family, faith, love, identity, growing up, and all the quiet moments in between. Not because there is always an answer, but because sometimes there is something worth saying even when there isn't.

Some things are easier to write when no one knows who is holding the pen.

Alex is the voice I gave to those things.

The thoughts I couldn't say out loud. The feelings I didn't have names for. The stories that deserved to exist, even if they were never meant to belong entirely to me.

Paper & Echo is where I let them exist.
